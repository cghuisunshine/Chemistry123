How can an isotherm estimate K and total capacity?

What does a half-covered surface tell us?

Use a direct physical landmark

θ = 1/2 ⇒ Kp = 1 ⇒ phalf = 1/K

A larger K means half coverage is reached at lower pressure at this temperature. Because K is defined here using pressure rather than a dimensionless gas activity, its units are inverse pressure.

Relate coverage to a measured uptake

V = VmaxKp/(1 + Kp) p/V = 1/(KVmax) + p/Vmax

If measured adsorbed volume V is proportional to coverage under a consistent reporting convention, Vmax is the monolayer capacity. A p/V versus p plot has slope 1/Vmax and intercept 1/(KVmax). A nonlinear fit to V(p) can be preferable when measurement errors are not uniform after transformation.
