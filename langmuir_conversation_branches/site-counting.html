Why do the rates contain θ and 1 − θ?

What is being counted by each kinetic factor?

Count empty and occupied sites

Let N be the fixed total number of equivalent adsorption sites. Nθ are occupied and N(1 − θ) are empty. Gas collisions occur at a frequency proportional to pressure at fixed temperature; only collisions at vacant sites can adsorb in the model.

adsorption rate ∝ pN(1 − θ) desorption rate ∝ Nθ

Cancel the same site count

Write the proportionality constants as ka and kd. Equating the rates and cancelling N gives ka p(1 − θ) = kd θ. The model assumes the desorption probability per occupied site does not depend on neighbouring occupancy. Interactions between adsorbates would invalidate that simple coefficient.
