Why is the simple isotherm specific to nondissociative adsorption?

Change the elementary event

For dissociative adsorption A₂(g) + 2* ⇌ 2A*, a simple independent-site mean-field model uses an adsorption factor p(1 − θ)² and a recombination-desorption factor θ². Adjacent-site statistics and interactions are neglected in this approximation.

kₐp(1 − θ)² = kdθ²

Solve the changed balance

θ/(1 − θ) = √(Kp) θ = √(Kp)/[1 + √(Kp)]

The square-root pressure dependence differs from the nondissociative Langmuir expression. A similar-looking surface problem therefore needs its own explicit site stoichiometry and rate model; one cannot automatically reuse θ = Kp/(1 + Kp).
