Why are liquid and vapour compositions different?

Does a liquid containing 40% A make vapour containing 40% A?

Add the component pressures

p = xApA* + (1 − xA)pB* yA = pA/p = xApA*/p

xA is a liquid mole fraction. yA is a vapour mole fraction, using the ideal-gas partial-pressure relation. The more volatile component is enriched in the vapour.

Work a binary example

Let xA = 0.40, pA* = 50 kPa and pB* = 20 kPa at the same temperature. Then pA = 20 kPa, pB = 12 kPa and total p = 32 kPa. The vapour has yA = 20/32 = 0.625. A 40% liquid composition therefore gives a 62.5% vapour composition in this model.
