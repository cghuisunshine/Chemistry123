Where does the liquid chemical-potential formula come from?

Is Raoult’s law proved without an ideal-solution model?

State the model explicitly

μA(l) = μA*(l) + RT ln xA

This is the ideal-solution premise used by the derivation. It may be motivated statistically by ideal mixing or established through measured solution behaviour. The present algebra does not establish that every real liquid follows it.

Read the free-energy lowering

For 0 < xA < 1, ln xA is negative. Mixing therefore lowers A’s chemical potential relative to pure A at the same conditions. Equilibrium with vapour can be restored at a lower A partial pressure. The formula applies to each component of an ideal solution, not just to the component in excess.
