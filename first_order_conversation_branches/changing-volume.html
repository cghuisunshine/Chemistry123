What if the reacting volume changes?

Why insist on fixed volume?

Differentiate the amount balance

Let nA = cV and suppose molecular disappearance remains first order in the amount, with dnA/dt = −knA. A moving boundary changes concentration even without changing molecule number.

V dc/dt + c dV/dt = −kcV dc/dt = −kc − (c/V)dV/dt

Integrate using the known volume history

c(t)/c₀ = [V₀/V(t)] e−kt

The exponential describes chemical survival. The volume ratio separately describes dilution or compression. For k = 0 and a doubling of volume, concentration halves even though no molecules react.

Specify which half-life you mean

The amount half-life remains ln 2/k under this amount-based model. The time for concentration to halve can differ because volume also changes. In a flow reactor, inflow and outflow add further terms; the closed-batch derivation cannot be transferred without a new balance.
