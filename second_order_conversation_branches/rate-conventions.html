Where do factors of two enter a second-order law?

What exactly does k measure?

Separate event rate from species disappearance

For 2A → products, one reaction event consumes two A molecules. The rate of reaction per volume is conventionally r = −(1/2)dc/dt. If a kinetic model gives r = krc², the measured disappearance law is:

−dc/dt = 2krc² ≡ kdc²

Integrate the law actually stated

1/c(t) = 1/c₀ + kdt t½ = 1/(kdc₀) = 1/(2krc₀)

The workbook defines k directly by disappearance, so its formula contains no extra 2. Either convention is acceptable if the definition of k is carried through every equation.

Why a quadratic dependence can arise

In an elementary well-mixed binary encounter model the number of possible A–A pairs scales as N(N − 1)/2. At large N and fixed density, the reaction-event rate per volume scales as c². The pair-count factor and event stoichiometry are absorbed into the stated rate constant. An overall balanced equation alone does not establish this model.
