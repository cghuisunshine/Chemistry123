How is activation energy different from reaction enthalpy?

Does an exothermic reaction necessarily have a small rate barrier?

Compare endpoints with a rate dependence

Reaction enthalpy compares product and reactant states. Arrhenius activation energy describes the temperature sensitivity of the rate constant. A reaction can release energy overall while proceeding slowly because a transition region is difficult to reach. A catalyst changes the kinetic pathway without changing the equilibrium free-energy difference.

Use a local definition beyond a straight line

Eₐ(T) = RT² d ln(k/kref)/dT

This derivative defines an apparent activation energy even when the Arrhenius plot is curved. A temperature-dependent prefactor or changing mechanism can cause such curvature. Treating this derivative as one constant over a large interval then introduces an extra approximation.
