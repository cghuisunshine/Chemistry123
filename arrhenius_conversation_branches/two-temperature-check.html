Predict a rate change without knowing A

How do signs and energy units affect the prediction?

Subtract the same model at two temperatures

ln(k₂/k₁) = −(Eₐ/R)(1/T₂ − 1/T₁)

For positive activation energy, raising T makes the inverse-temperature difference negative and the logarithmic rate ratio positive. Use Kelvin and keep Ea consistent with the energy unit in R.

Calculate and interpret the ratio

For Ea = 50,000 J mol⁻¹, T₁ = 300 K and T₂ = 310 K, the logarithmic ratio is approximately 0.647 and k₂/k₁ is approximately 1.91. This is a prediction for k. It is the same factor for reaction rate only if the concentration factors and other rate-law conditions are held fixed.
