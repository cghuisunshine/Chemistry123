What does the sign of the boundary slope mean?

Why can melting have a negative pressure slope?

Compare the competing preferences

An endothermic transition has positive ΔHₘ and hence positive ΔSₘ at coexistence. Higher temperature favours that higher-entropy phase. Increasing pressure favours the phase with smaller molar volume. These preferences determine whether pressure must rise or fall to compensate for an increase in temperature.

dp/dT = ΔHₘ/(TΔVₘ)

Use the same transition direction

For ice melting near ordinary pressure, liquid water has smaller molar volume than ice. Thus ΔfusVₘ is negative, while ΔfusHₘ is positive. The melting curve has negative slope. Reversing the transition changes the signs of both ΔHₘ and ΔVₘ, leaving the ratio unchanged.

(J mol⁻¹)/(K m³ mol⁻¹) = Pa K⁻¹

The reciprocal slope dT/dp has units K Pa⁻¹. Do not exchange these slopes without taking a reciprocal.
