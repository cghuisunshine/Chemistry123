Why replace the transition volume by RT/p?

Which physical approximation creates the simple law?

Start with an exact difference

ΔvapVₘ = Vₘ,g − Vₘ,l

Vaporization replaces a mole of liquid by a mole of vapour. At low vapour density the gas molar volume is much larger than the liquid molar volume. Neglecting the latter gives ΔVₘ ≈ Vₘ,g.

Make a separate gas approximation

Vₘ,g ≈ RT/p dp/dT ≈ pΔvapHₘ/(RT²)

The ideal-vapour approximation is distinct from neglecting liquid volume. Both can fail near a critical point, where liquid and vapour densities approach one another. The exact Clapeyron equation remains the starting point when these substitutions fail.
