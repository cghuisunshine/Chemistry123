How is Gibbs energy related to useful work?

What changes if the system delivers electricity?

Retain the non-PV work term

At fixed T and P with a closed system and a thermal reservoir at T, let wnon-PV,on denote work on the system other than PV work. The first law gives q = ΔH − wnon-PV,on.

ΔSuniv = ΔS − (ΔH − wnon-PV,on)/T ΔG = wnon-PV,on − TΔSuniv

Read the work bound with the sign convention

wnon-PV,out = −wnon-PV,on ≤ −ΔG

A spontaneous decrease in Gibbs energy sets the maximum non-PV work output. Equality requires zero entropy generation, the reversible limit. This is the origin of the relation ΔᵣG = −nFE for a reversible electrochemical cell.

Spontaneity does not predict speed

A negative Gibbs driving force indicates which direction is thermodynamically allowed under the constraints. It does not remove an activation barrier. A metastable mixture can persist for a long time despite a lower-G state being available.

For a reacting mixture, the local criterion uses ΔᵣG = (∂G/∂ξ)T,P. The total finite ΔG is an integral of that changing derivative.
