Is this a derivation of quantum mechanics?

Which statements are postulates rather than algebra?

Separate a physical model from its consequences

The wavefunction description, the energy-eigenstate condition and the momentum operator are quantum premises. Combining the specified operators produces the displayed differential equation, but this construction does not prove the premises from Newton’s laws. Experiments motivate and test the quantum theory.

Connect stationary and dynamical equations

iℏ ∂Ψ/∂t = ĤΨ Ψ(x,t) = ψ(x)exp(−iEt/ℏ) Ĥψ = Eψ

For a time-independent Hamiltonian, insert this separated form into the time-dependent postulate. The common time factor cancels. Its magnitude is one, so |Ψ|² = |ψ|² is independent of time for this single energy eigenstate. A superposition can have time-dependent probability density.
