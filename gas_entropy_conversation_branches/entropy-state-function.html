The actual expansion is irreversible—what now?

Distinguish the definition from actual heat

Entropy is a state function defined between equilibrium states by integrating δqrev/T on a reversible calculation path. The actual process need not follow that path. This is analogous to evaluating a conservative potential difference along a mathematically convenient route.

S₂ − S₁ = ∫1→2, rev δq/T

Account for entropy production

For a closed system exchanging heat with a boundary temperature Tb, entropy accounting separates transfer from generation. With multiple contacts, sum the transfer terms.

ΔS = ∫ δqactual/Tb + Sgen Sgen ≥ 0

Insulation removes the transfer term; it does not require Sgen = 0. Free expansion is a direct example.

Choose two easy reversible legs

To connect (T₁,V₁) to (T₂,V₂), first heat reversibly at fixed V₁, then expand reversibly at fixed T₂. The first leg gives n∫Cᵥ,ₘ(T)dT/T and the second gives nR ln(V₂/V₁). Their sum equals the entropy change for every path with those endpoints.
